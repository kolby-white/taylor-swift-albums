Revision notes

The original StoryMap is a clear, well-ordered discography with good imagery. These are the changes that would make it stronger, ranked by impact. Each one notes what this revised edition does about it.

1

Put maps in the StoryMap biggest gap

The original has no maps, but it is built in a mapping platform, and the subject is full of geography: a world tour, a move from Pennsylvania to Nashville, and real places named in the lyrics.

Done here: an Eras Tour map of all 149 shows, sized by number of shows and filterable by leg, plus a "Where the songs come from" map of nine places. In ArcGIS you could rebuild these as a Map Tour block and an Express Map, or as a web map from a CSV of the tour cities.

2

Write in your own voice

Almost every paragraph is a Wikipedia quote. This makes the story read like a reference page and adds little to the source. A short original take on each album, such as what changed, why it matters, or one song to start with, gives readers a reason to be there.

Done here: each album leads with a two-sentence summary, genre, label and key tracks. The Wikipedia text is kept, credited, in a collapsible "Source excerpt".

3

Give the story a thread

As it stands, the story is a list. Swift's career has a clear plot that ties the sections together: genre reinvention, and the fight to own her work, which ends with her buying back her masters.

Done here: a new intro and headline stats, a clickable timeline that shows the switch from Big Machine to Republic, and a "Who owns the music?" section with a dated timeline.

4

Update the re-recordings section accuracy

The section implies the re-recording project is still underway. In May 2025 Swift bought back the original masters of all six Big Machine albums, so Taylor Swift and Reputation were never re-recorded. The original also doesn't give the release dates of the four Taylor's Versions.

Done here: the section now covers 2018–2025 and gives each re-recording's date. Album cards show "Taylor's Version" badges or a "never re-recorded" note.

5

Use the navigation and credits blocks

The StoryMap contains a navigation block with no links and a credits section with an empty heading, paragraph and attribution. Readers can't jump between sections, and no images are attributed.

Done here: a sticky section menu, and a full credits section covering text licensing (Wikipedia is CC BY-SA), image rights and map data.

6

Fix small consistency issues

"The Tortured Poet's Department" caption should be "Poets". Some albums have a gallery of both versions and others a single image, with no explanation. The stylized lowercase titles (folklore, evermore, reputation) are used inconsistently. The debut cover is hotlinked from People.com and will break if that page changes; upload it to the story as you did with the other images.

Done here: captions corrected and standardized, both covers labelled with the year, and stylization noted once per album.

7

Add alt text and accessibility

The images have captions but no alternative text, and the 2019 CBS interview is a still image rather than a video.

Done here: descriptive alt text on every image, keyboard-accessible controls, dark-mode support, and a layout that works on phones. Still to do: embed the CBS clip (StoryMaps supports YouTube embeds) and a playable track for each album.

8

Ideas for further expansion

Add an Eras Tour economics map (local spending, hotel and attendance records by city) using choropleth shading. Map the surprise songs played in each city, or chart album length and track counts over time. Use a sidecar block per era with that era's color for a more immersive read, and add a short conclusion connecting The Life of a Showgirl back to the debut.

Note: the 2026 section (The Encore and "Patient Zero") is carried over from the original story with its Genius, MSN and Capital FM sourcing. It was not independently re-checked.
